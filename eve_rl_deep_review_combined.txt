B. Design-decision inventory (currently in force)
Reward (env5, post-Plan-v9; refs RL_IMPROV_9 §3 table + RL_IMPROV_10 §3–6): 1. TargetReached +3 —
justification: success must clearly dominate short failures (spec §7). Tested via reward-distribution separation;
solid. 2. ArcLengthProgress +0.01/mm, gated on is_on_correct_path(); off-path = −0.01×Δ(off-arc)
symmetric delta (retraction earns positive) — justification: heuristic/reward agreement (RL_IMPROV_9 §1); the
delta-vs-cumulative choice was corrected after an asymmetry calculation, not an experiment. 2× progress
inside target daughter moving forward (RL_IMPROV_10 §5) — replaces a dwell-rewarding constant;
reasoning sound, untested in isolation. 3. Lateral penalty −0.001×max(0, cross_track − local_tolerance)
(radius-aware deadband, RL_IMPROV_10 §6) — justification: 5mm trunk wall-riding is unavoidable, was
−3/episode drag. Verified as part of the inversion fix. 4. +1 per on-path junction commit, latched
(RL_IMPROV_10 §3) — justification: dense credit for each fork decision; accepted side effect: partial-fail nets
+1.7 (positive). Verified only via reward-ordering (+3..+7 ≫  +1.7 ≫  +0.2). 5. −0.05 on deep wrong-commit
(≥10mm off-arc since divergence, latched per divergence) — tuned specifically to survive the RVA/RCCA
tolerance-asymmetry flicker. Magnitude arbitrary. 6. Path-segment step penalty (trunk −0.007→−0.002
interpolated; (0) −0.002; bridge/daughter 0.0; off-path −0.007; RL_IMPROV_10 §4) — RCCA-topology-specific
(regex on branch names \(2\), \(0\), \(11\)); portability to other daughters unstated. 7.
FAILURE_TRUNCATION_PENALTY −5 on wbt/fold/vessel-end, not max_steps (spec §6, RL_IMPROV_4 §6) —
rationale: early aborts remove future step penalties. Long-standing, never re-examined after the step penalty
changed shape in §4 (max_steps episodes no longer accumulate ~−1 uniformly).
Termination/truncation semantics: 8. All truncations set terminated=True ("absorbing failure state",
RL_IMPROV_9 §17) — the single most load-bearing fix; justified by batch evidence (0/13 failure ends
grounded) and validated by subsequent critic stability. Note this makes truncation-vs-termination semantically
meaningless downstream and bakes "failure is unrecoverable" into the value function. 9. Truncation triggers:
wrong_branch_timeout at 50 consecutive off-path steps; fold-stall (insertion without arclength progress);
vessel-end; sim-error; max_steps. Grace values (50, 15–20) empirically tuned per RL_IMPROV_7 Fix 3 and never
revisited after the state machine replaced the debounce.
Observation (76-dim, RL_IMPROV_9 §5): 10. Tracking2D(10, 15) + Memory(2, FILL) (velocity is genuine
hidden state — Memory was removed in IMPROV_4/7 then re-added), target 2D, last_action 4,
inserted_lengths 2, guidance 28. Justified by the §32 audit (B1/B2/B3) + 18-item non-Markov audit; never
ablated. 11. Guidance includes a phase one-hot read from _heur_rva_phase (RL_IMPROV_9 §4) — defined
by the heuristic's presence; during pure RL exploration/eval it reads a getattr default. No doc examines what
the policy sees for this feature at eval time. Weakly justified. 12. Branch state machine (radius-aware tolerance
K=1.5, commit hysteresis 10mm, on-path masks) is simultaneously the classifier for reward, heuristic gating,
and observation features — a deliberate unification, protected by the standing rule (RL_IMPROV_10
preamble).
Algorithm & replay: 13. AWAC (λ=3.0) over SAC — chosen because every SAC variant diverged; validated
(critic stable 750k+ steps, RL_IMPROV_10 §14). λ=3 untuned; raising to 6–10 is an explicitly untried lever
(§19.2). 14. Step replay (1e6–11M transitions, batch 256, update/explore = 1.0) + PER (α 0.6, β 0.4→1.0) + --
balanced_fraction clean-lane sampler (0.6 for LCCA) + demo_priority_bonus. PER canonical values, never
A/B'd against uniform despite the doc claiming the design permits clean attribution (RL_IMPROV_9 §14). 15.
grad_clip 1.0 — despite RL_IMPROV_9 §16 arguing for 10.0 ("1.0 would clip every healthy update too");
RL_IMPROV_10 §12 reports 1.0 "barely binds". No doc records the change decision. 16. MLP 256×256, no
embedder (LSTM dropped; ff_only path avoided due to latent bug), lr 3e-4, 10k warm-start pretrain, update
budget from exploration steps only (RL_IMPROV_9 §13, §15). 17. Entropy handling: log_std_min −2 floor +
log_std_max 0.0 ceiling (HANDOFF EXP-1). Floor demonstrably insufficient (RL_IMPROV_10 §15); ceiling
validated for the ceiling-explosion mode only. No entropy term in the AWAC loss — the actual fix (explicit
entropy bonus / tanh-margin penalty) is still deferred.
Architecture & training strategy: 18. Per-daughter policies (not a shared model) — argued in RL_IMPROV_8
§35 (seeding imbalance 30×, distinct Phase-B/C steering, baselines needed); the shared-model + per-target
buffers + PER/HER stack (§36) was never run. 19. Heuristic warm-start pipeline: per-daughter phase-based
heuristic (arclength-windowed Phase A/B/C, closed-loop J-tip bend rotation, original sign convention —
empirically validated in the v2–v8 grid, RL_IMPROV_8 §22), branch-balanced (seed, options) schedule, min-
success-rate collection with ≤70% success cap, clean_thread = final-branch equality (RL_IMPROV_10 §2),
is_demo tagging. 20. Start-state strategy: RCCA = restore-at-fork from 5 curated SOFA checkpoints (fuller 7-
field restore, settle 3); LCCA = z=345 no restore ("Challenge-1"). Explicitly two opposing regimes; per-state
difficulty is known to dominate the metric (§16). 21. Eval protocol: deterministic mean-action, 98 fixed seeds,
every 250k explore steps; "Quality" = strict TargetReached fraction; eval env wraps with the same restore
wrapper as train (RL_IMPROV_10 §13d) — except in the LCCA campaign where there's no restore. "Functional
success" (R ∈ [−1,1]) exists as a secondary lens but Quality remains the headline. 22. Standing rules
(RL_IMPROV_10 §11, §19; HANDOFF §7): never modify the branch classifier to fix reward; one variable under
study per iteration; reward/obs/terminal/dynamics changes require explicit human approval.
C. Contradictions & smells
Direct reversals of earlier fixes: 1. Heuristic controller triple-reversal: HEURISTIC_FAILURE_FIX spec
§"Controller Changes" mandates removing the max(gw_trans, 5.0) floor, adding a device-length-aware cap,
and adding a rotation cap near target. RL_IMPROV_4 §11 does the exact opposite of all three (re-adds the
5mm floor, removes the rotation cap, removes the device-length cap) with a one-paragraph rationale. No
experiment cited for either direction. 2. Memory wrapper: removed as unnecessary ("wire shape is sufficient",
RL_IMPROV_4 P4, RL_IMPROV_7 §1) → re-added as necessary ("velocity is genuine hidden state",
RL_IMPROV_9 §5). Tracking config churned 3pts×2mm → 5×10 → 10×15 with a cache-invalidation cost each
time. 3. Hysteresis/debounce lineage: debounce added (IMPROV_7 Fix 2) → made obsolete by state machine
("no separate debouncer needed", IMPROV_8 §14) → left as dead code (IMPROV_9 §6 item 14) → a new
debounce attempted and reverted (IMPROV_10 §11.4) because it broke the heuristic. The same flicker problem
was attacked five separate ways (proxy threshold, interior points, debounce, state machine + radius tolerance,
reward-side latch). 4. HER: recommended repeatedly (RL_Improv_2 §4.2, RL_Improv_3 §10) and analyzed as
"huge for LCCA — its 1.4% becomes effectively 80%+" (RL_IMPROV_8 §36), then categorically ruled out
(RL_IMPROV_9 §14: reward is path-structured, state unreconstructable). The §36 analysis was written after the
path-structured reward already existed — the contradiction is between two analyses of the same code, one of
which is wrong. 5. grad_clip 10.0 vs 1.0 (RL_IMPROV_9 §16 vs RL_IMPROV_10 §12/HANDOFF §5): the earlier
doc explicitly rejects 1.0 with a quantitative argument; the working stack uses 1.0 with no recorded rationale
for the switch. 6. --insertion_z / --checkpoint_dir mutual exclusion (IMPROV_8 §1) later relaxed
(IMPROV_10 §8b) — fine, but the guard's original justification ("opposing strategies") was quietly invalidated.
7. OFF_BRANCH_GRACE_STEPS 10 → 20 → 50: each change compensated for a side effect of another fix
(spec §3 says "recoveries happen in 2–7 steps, 10 covers those"; the debounce then made 20 lethal to slow
recoveries; 50 chosen). The parameter encodes the history of other bugs rather than a principled quantity.
Fixes fighting fixes / whack-a-mole reward engineering: 8. Wrong-branch penalties: −1 entry + −0.1/step
added (IMPROV_4 §5) → 3-step grace bolted on for flicker (IMPROV_8 §29) → entire cascade deleted as
"heuristic-era hacks" (IMPROV_9 §3). Correct-entry +1: proximity-gated (IMPROV_7 §12) → daughter-fork-
gated (IMPROV_8 §6) → state-machine ±1 events (IMPROV_9 §2) → +1 at every junction with a −0.05 deep-
commit penalty (IMPROV_10 §3). Five reward regimes in four docs for the same behavioral target. 9.
Magnitude inconsistency inside that lineage: IMPROV_9 §2 specifies −1 for a wrong-daughter commit;

A. Bellman target / done-flag flow (checklist 1)
A1. CONCEPTUAL — truncated → terminated=True grounds time-limit truncations as absorbing states.
training _scripts\util\env5.py:1422-1423 rewrites terminated=True for every truncation (wrong-branch
timeout, fold stall, vessel end, sim error, and max_steps). The buffer stores only episode.terminals
(replaybuffer.py:78-84; truncations are dropped at to_replay()), and the critic target masks with it:
sac.py:587 expected_q = rewards + (1 - dones) * gamma * next_target_q. Consequence: for a pure time-
limit truncation the target is r with no bootstrap, so Q at late-episode states learns "the world ends here" —

and the −5 FailureTruncationPenalty fires first, but max_steps and sim_error are not MDP failures. Fix:
keep terminated=True for the failure-classified truncations only; for max_steps/sim_error store

the standard TimeLimit handling). Because Episode.truncations never reaches the buffer, this distinction
currently cannot be recovered downstream.

B. AWAC actor loss (checklist 2)
B1. CONCEPTUAL/BUG-risk — log_prob floor clamp kills the BC gradient for exactly the demos AWAC
needs most. network\gaussianpolicy.py:76: return log_prob.sum(-1, keepdim=True).clamp(min=-20.0).
clamp has zero gradient in the clamped region, so any buffer action whose log-prob under the current policy
is below −20 contributes zero policy gradient (the AWAC term -(weight*logp) in sac.py:446 becomes a
constant). With 4 action dims and log_std railed at −2 (std≈0.135), a demo ~3σ from the mean per dim
already sums below −20. Failure mode: once the policy drifts away from the demo manifold, the high-
advantage demos silently stop pulling it back — a self-reinforcing freeze that would look like "AWAC stopped
learning" while awac_weight_* diagnostics still look fine (the weights are computed from Q, not logp). Fix:
clamp with a straight-through/soft floor (e.g. -softplus(-(logp+20))-20), or clamp per-dim z distance
instead, and log the clamp-hit fraction.

C. log_std_min / log_std_max caps (checklist 3)
C1. CONCEPTUAL (high relevance to the v2 experiment) — hard torch.clamp makes both rails sticky.
gaussianpolicy.py:44,53: log_std = torch.clamp(log_std, min, max). Gradient is exactly zero outside the
band, so once the pre-clamp head output crosses a rail, no loss term can push it back directly — not the
alpha*log_pi entropy term, not the per-dim entropy betas (which act on the clamped value, sac.py:475-
482), nothing. Only incidental trunk drift can un-rail it. With the original (−20, 2) band this rarely matters; with
the deliberately narrow (−2, 0) band of launch_lcca_awac_v2.sh:100-101 railing is expected (the v1 forensic
saw log_std → +2 on all dims — i.e. hard-railed at the old max). The new max=0 cap will stop the variance
explosion, but by saturation, not regulation: pre-clamp values will pile up above 0 with zero restoring gradient,
and the "log_std" telemetry will read a frozen 0.0. Fix: rescale instead of clamp — log_std = log_std_min + 
0.5*(log_std_max−log_std_min)*(tanh(x)+1) (SAC-v2 style) so gradient exists everywhere; entropy/log-prob

D1. BUG (low frequency) — stale-index priority updates can hit ring-overwritten or prefetch-raced

forward motion in the target daughter earns 2×pf·Δs, but the matching backward motion is charged only
1×pf·Δs. Every forward-back oscillation cycle nets +pf·Δs for zero net progress, and a ≥0.5mm wiggle never
trips the fold-stall detector. This is a direct positive incentive for shallow dithering inside the daughter — the
exact failure you named "shallow-stall." The fix is to make depth potential-based: credit only new maximum
arclength reached (pf·max(0, s − s_max_so_far)), which is un-farmable by construction.
2.2 Timing out with banked reward is unpenalized — stalling is locally optimal [verified]. env5.py:1073-
1088: the −5 failure penalty gates on vessel-end, fold-stall, or off-branch timeout. A pure max_steps
truncation pays nothing — while the episode has already banked up to +3 in junction commits plus ~+4 of
(doubled) progress shaping. So "enter the daughter, dither, coast to timeout" ends around +7 with zero risk,
while pushing for the siphon target risks vessel-end (−5, see 2.3) for a +3 bonus. The expected-value-
maximizing policy stops short of the target. Combined with 2.1, this is the single strongest structural reason
success caps below 100%.
2.3 Overshooting the correct daughter is punished like entering a wrong one. A wire that threads
correctly but runs to the vessel end trips VesselEnd → −5 (the soft −1 overshoot path only fires in the off-
branch-timeout branch, env5.py:1051-1087). Together, 2.1–2.3 say: approach is generously paid, stopping is
free, and going deep is the only penalized action. Your reward currently teaches conservatism.
2.4 Neutral policy output = +10 mm/s forward on both devices [verified]. Translation limits are [−10,
+30] and the runner maps [−1,1] linearly (single.py:113-116), so action 0 — where a tanh-Gaussian
initializes and where entropy regularization pulls the mean — commands +10 mm/s insertion on guidewire
and catheter. Holding still requires outputting −0.5. A built-in catheter-forward bias plus finding 2.5 is a clean
mechanistic account of the siphon catheter-overshoot fold. Fix: center the mapping (or bias the policy-mean
initialization to the hold action).
2.5 The catheter is controlled semi-blind. All pose features in the 30-dim LocalGuidance derive from the
guidewire tracking; the catheter appears only as an inserted-length scalar and last-action. There is no catheter
tip position, heading, or cross-track feature, even though fluoroscopy.device_trackings3d exists unused. The
agent must infer catheter-tip state at the siphon — precisely where it must coordinate hold/retract against
guidewire advance. This confirms your own EXP-3 hypothesis from the code side: the missing observability is
real, and it's on the catheter, not just the fork.
2.6 All 16 workers explore an identical target sequence [verified mechanism]. centerlinerandom.py:34
constructs random.Random() once in the parent process; workers receive deepcopies of that RNG state, and
unseeded explore resets never reseed it (centerlinerandom.py:40-42). The Plan-v12 spawn reseed fixed the
global RNGs only. Your effective explore-target diversity is ~1/16 of nominal and PER receives 16-way-
correlated data — right now, in the live container. (Eval and heuristic seeding are unaffected: both pass explicit
seeds.) One-line fix in the spawn reseed block.
3. Bugs — Tier 2: the AWAC learning machinery
3.1 The auto-entropy "anti-rail" mechanism self-disables [verified, my own derivation]. target_entropy
= −4 (sac.py:210) but your healthy policy runs at entropy ≈ +2.65. The alpha loss therefore pushes
log_alpha down monotonically — alpha decays exponentially toward zero and the +α·log_pi term in the
AWAC loss (the "Plan v11 PRIMARY anti-rail mechanism," sac.py:437-446) vanishes early in training. It can
only revive once entropy has already collapsed below −4, i.e. after the policy is deeply railed. It is a lagging
regulator with a huge dead zone — consistent with v2 still sliding 59%→43% late in the run. Fix: set
target_entropy near the healthy operating point (≈ +1 to +2 for this action dim and std band), or better,
regulate what you actually care about — drive alpha with a setpoint controller on clamp_fraction (you
already log it).
3.2 Hard torch.clamp on log_std makes both rails sticky [verified]. gaussianpolicy.py:44: outside the
band the gradient is exactly zero, so no loss term can pull a railed log_std back — only incidental trunk drift
can. This is the actual mechanism of v1's one-way "ceiling explosion," and it means EXP-1 (log_std_max 0.0)
works by saturation, not regulation — pre-clamp values pile up above the cap with no restoring force. The
standard fix is the SAC-v2 soft parameterization: log_std = min + 0.5·(max−min)·(tanh(x)+1).
3.3 The −20 log-prob floor zeroes the BC gradient on exactly the demos you need [verified].
gaussianpolicy.py:76: when the policy is confidently wrong (σ near the floor, mean far from a demo), the
demo's log-prob falls below −20, the clamp flattens it, and the gradient is exactly zero. In AWAC the
weighted-BC term is the only improvement signal, so "confidently wrong at bif2" is a frozen state — the hook
demos in your clean lane silently stop teaching. This plausibly explains both the v1 mean-never-locking-the-
hook symptom and v2's long ~25% plateau before breakout. Fix: a leaky floor (e.g. −20 + 0.05·(logp+20)
below the knee, or −softplus-based) so direction survives with damped magnitude — and log the clamp-hit
fraction.
3.4 Every network is one hidden layer, not two [verified]. mlp.py:83-87 has no activation between the
input layer and the first hidden layer — the author even left # TODO: Add F.relu after input layer. Linear ∘ Linear
collapses to a single affine map, so your "256×256" policy and critics are effectively single-hidden-layer nets.
Everything ever trained in the MLP era, including the 86% RCCA stack, ran at half the intended depth. The fix is
one line but changes the function class — old checkpoints won't be comparable and can't be warm-started
meaningfully.
3.5 Assorted, real but smaller: exploration noise is a scalar added to all 4 dims (perfectly correlated across
devices) and unclipped beyond [−1,1] (sac.py:235); PER beta never reaches 1.0 (anneal denominator is
explore steps, advance is per update — ends ~0.7 at 20M, ~0.43 at your 2M horizon); reward_scaling is
plumbed everywhere and applied nowhere; the padding-mask path biases the alpha loss and AWAC
diagnostics (episode-mode only); a first-update-after-warm-start uses stale alpha=1.0; the SAC branch's non-
detached alpha survives only by fragile zero_grad ordering.
4. Bugs — Tier 3: environment internals and eval integrity
State-machine one-step lag: update_branch_state() runs after reward and observation each step, and the
is_on_correct_path cache isn't invalidated after the update — so the progress-vs-off-path switch, the −0.007
off-path tax, the off-branch counter, and obs features 19/25–28 all act on the previous step's classification
while using the current projection. At fork-decision moments the agent's "which branch am I in" bits describe
where it was, not where it is.
Hairpin-blind projection: polyline.py:82-144 takes a global nearest-segment argmin with no continuity
window. At the carotid siphon the ascending/descending limbs are adjacent, so tip jitter flips the projection
between limbs, spiking Δs by the loop length — reward, d_rem, tangent, and curvature features are noisiest
exactly where fine control matters most. Fix: window the search to ±30mm of the previous arclength (pure
geometry, doesn't touch the classifier).
Success without entry: targets sample uniformly over the whole daughter centerline including the ostium;
success is a 5mm Euclidean ball, so a proximally-sampled target can be "reached" from the bridge or a sister
branch. Quality mildly over-counts.
Eval integrity: a worker crash mid-eval nulls the seed lists before collection — the restarted worker plays one
unseeded random episode and the "98-seed Quality" silently becomes a ~91-episode number with noise
mixed in. Separately, at p≈0.5 your 98-episode eval has ±5pp standard error: the 43→59→43 swing is ~2–3σ
and best_checkpoint argmax over that metric has winner's-curse bias — the "59.2%" is best read as "low-to-
mid 50s."
The per-eval buffer dump: _replay_save_interval=1 exports the entire replay buffer via uncompressed
np.savez at every 250k eval. With the 11M buffer this grows toward ~7GB per eval, doubles buffer-subprocess
RAM during the np.stack, and blocks training synchronously. Calibrated for the RCCA 1M buffer, never
revisited.
Latent traps: buffer save deadlocks any non-PER run at first eval (export_all unimplemented on uniform
buffers); with any nonzero embedder_layers the shared embedder is stepped only by q1's optimizer while
receiving gradients from all three losses; deterministic eval runs forward_play twice per step (corrupts hidden
state if an LSTM ever returns); training resume never restores buffer/runner state and the update-budget
formula stalls resumed runs.

7. Overlooked and alternative strategies — the "next level" roadmap
Ordered by expected leverage per unit of risk:
7.1 Fix the MDP first, and build the missing safety net (highest leverage, near-zero research
risk)
Everything in Tier 1 (2.1–2.6) plus the projection window. But the durable upgrade is a reward property-test
suite — the docs show five reward regimes in four generations, each breaking invariants the previous one
established (the list.add latch bug alone silently ate the +1 signal for 2,544 episodes). Encode invariants as
replay tests over golden episodes: no closed loop in state space nets positive reward; oscillation nets ≤0;
success > any failure; ordering success ≫  partial ≫  dwell ≫  deep-wrong; terminal penalties fire on every
truncation class. Run on every reward edit. This converts your recurring whack-a-mole into a regression wall.
Important consequence: fixing 2.1/2.2 changes the reward function → lcca_awac_seed_v1.npz rewards go
stale. You already have the precedent and tooling (the RL_IMPROV_9 §17 retro-patched caches): re-score the
stored trajectories offline rather than re-harvesting from SOFA.
7.2 Revisit HER — the ruling-out was wrong, and it attacks your #1 bottleneck
RL_IMPROV_9 rejected HER because "the reward is path-structured and per-episode state is
unreconstructable." That conflates goal relabeling with reward reconstruction. The path-context state machine
is a deterministic function of (tip trajectory, planned path) — and you already re-run rewards over stored
trajectories offline. So: for every episode that ended in the "wrong" daughter, re-run the state machine offline
with that daughter as the goal → a success demo for that goal. Your 38% LVA-confusion failures become an
LVA success dataset for free. Sparse per-daughter successes are your single scarcest resource; HER is the data
multiplier purpose-built for it. This pairs naturally with:
7.3 One goal-conditioned policy across daughters
Condition on (daughter one-hot, target depth/arclength) instead of four separate policies. The trunk skill, the
arch, bif2 — all shared; the hook and the fork commits become goal-dependent behavior of one network. This
also makes the siphon problem a depth-conditioning problem you can curriculum over, and every daughter's
data regularizes the others. Fine-tune per-daughter afterwards if a daughter lags. The infrastructure (per-
target buffers, target-branch schedule, multi-target harvest from Plan v12) already exists.
7.4 Residual RL over your heuristic — you built the perfect substrate and aren't using it
You have per-daughter heuristics with ~92% LCCA reach. Instead of seeding a from-scratch policy with
heuristic demos, let the policy output a bounded delta on the heuristic's action (a = a_heur + πθ(s), clipped).
The exploration problem collapses from "learn to navigate from z=345" to "learn corrections where the
heuristic fails" — the fork hook and the siphon. This is the standard trick in contact-rich robot manipulation
for exactly this situation, and it composes with everything above. (One caution: the heuristic reads
is_on_correct_path, so the standing classifier-freeze rule still applies.)
7.5 Modernize the critic instead of leaning on the grounding hack
truncated→terminated for time-limits was a pragmatic fix for critic divergence, but it teaches the critic that
late-episode healthy states are worthless — and your own functional-success analysis proves many truncated
states are recoverable near-successes. The modern replacements for "value divergence in off-policy
continuous control" are cheap and well-validated: LayerNorm in the critics (single biggest stabilizer in recent
literature — CrossQ, DroQ lineage), critic ensembles / DroQ-style dropout-ensembles to kill overestimation,
and n-step (3–5) returns to propagate your dense shaping along 600-step episodes faster. With those in place,
restore correct time-limit bootstrapping for max_steps (keep terminated=True for genuinely stuck failure
truncations — that part of the grounding is defensible), and add a time-remaining observation feature to
resolve the aliasing properly.
7.6 Asymmetric actor-critic — you're sitting on privileged state and not using it
SOFA knows everything: full wire node positions, contact forces, catheter tip pose. Train the critic (and only
the critic) on privileged state while the policy uses the deployable observation. Better value targets, no
deployment cost. This directly attacks "critic quality," which your entire stabilization saga has been fighting
with blunter tools. Natural extension: a teacher policy trained with privileged obs + restore-anywhere
curriculum, distilled into the deployable-obs student (this is how most sim-trained navigation/locomotion
results get their final performance).
7.7 Curriculum, formalized — you already proved it works (+53pp) and then didn't
systematize it
Restore-at-fork was the single most effective intervention in the whole history. Generalize it: reverse
curriculum from the target backwards (start states sampled progressively further from the goal as success
rises), and a bandit over start-states/target-bands driven by your per-state success tables (sample where
learning progress is highest, not uniformly over a hard-skewed distribution). The siphon-band bias you
proposed in §19.4 is one cell of this; do the whole grid automatically.
7.8 Simplification candidates (subtract, don't add)
The sampler is currently PER × balanced-two-stream × demo-bonus — a triple-biased estimator whose IS
correction is provably not converging to unbiased (agent finding D4) and was never A/B'd against uniform.
Given AWAC's own weighting already does prioritization-by-advantage, try uniform sampling + the clean lane
as the ablation before tuning PER further. Similarly, the 30-dim guidance vector has never had a feature
ablation at any size (8→14→28→30); with two of its features provably stale-lagged and one heuristic-phase
feature that's semantically undefined during RL exploration, an ablation would likely both simplify and
improve.
7.9 Bigger swings, if you want research upside
Wire dynamics are hysteretic (contact state, friction, stored bending energy) — a genuine POMDP. Options in
ascending ambition: longer frame-stack on tip history; a small GRU (now safe post-step-RL, if J3's double-
forward is fixed); a learned dynamics model for short-horizon MPC or Dreamer-style imagination — at ~6
explore steps/s aggregate, your sim is expensive enough that model-based amplification has real ROI;
distributional critics (QR-DQN-style) for the heavy-tailed outcome structure. These are "after the
fundamentals" items.
