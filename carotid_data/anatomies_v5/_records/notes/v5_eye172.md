# v5_eye172: by-eye checks of the flagged 172-series pilot sides, the two reserves, and the final eligible list

State: `/sp/v5_va/state/v5_eye172.md`. All figures and JSON are in `/sp/v5_va/results/v5_eye172/`.

## Answer so far (2026-10-02, about 14:40; complete)

**All 7 pending 172-series sides are genuine VAs and are kept. Both reserves are confirmed as reserves; neither is
rejected outright. The final list is 27 eligible pairs: 25 primary (one per TopBrain patient) and 2 reserves.**

1. **Eye checks.** For each side I looked at the existing QC PNG and at my own CT look. The look has two straightened
   CPRs and 18 axial native-CT crops from the origin to the dura, plus HU/CCA along the trace (`v5_eye172_look.py`).
   - 058 L, 039 R, 039 L, 015 R, 190 R, 190 L and 141 R all follow the same course:
     - leave the subclavian
     - run V1 behind the CCA
     - pass the C6..C2 foramina inside a bony ring, then the C1 foramen
     - loop over the C1 posterior arch (V3)
     - enter the dura.
   - Each has a continuous lumen at HU/CCA about 0.9-1.1. None has a carotid shortcut, a vein shortcut or vein contact.
   - Every listed bridge on 039 and 190 is at the basilar confluence, above the splice.
   - 015 R enters at the C7 foramen, a known variant. It still follows the foraminal course and keeps its flag.
   - 039 L has a robust V3 excursion of 13.1 mm, but the loop is plainly visible on the axial crops.
   - Recorded as 7 x `"verdict": "kept"` (by v5_eye172) in `results/scaleup_eye_rejections.json`, appended only; the
     entry count went from 41 to 48. A backup of the file as it was is in `results/v5_eye172/`.
2. **Reserves.**
   - **ia172_172 L** (look plus `origin_ia172_172_L{,_low}.png`): this left VA arises **from the aortic arch** between
     the LCCA and the LSA (top of the arch at z about -731). It runs up as a separate 4 mm vessel next to the LSA.
     - The trace "origin" is a link of about 2 mm through tissue from the LSA to that vessel at z -715. Native HU along
       s 0-2 mm is 297, 154, 79, 125, 218. This is the verifier's "no enhanced SCA->VA link".
     - From s of about 3 mm the course is a true VA (C6..C1, V3 loop, HU/CCA 1.0).
     - **Reserve confirmed.** If it is used: advance the ostium at least 3 mm past the link and flag "arch-origin donor
       VA; subclavian junction synthetic".
   - **Song AHMU1218138**: both traces are VAs from origin to C1 (look_*), and the L wall is complete.
     - The R trace crosses the V3 loop above C1 by a 9.5 mm straight bridge at 10 HU, plus a 3 mm gap at C2 (221 HU =
       0.28 x CCA). The R wall ends at C2 (s 131 of 209).
     - Both walls are smoother than the dense host mesh: all-bins 0.0154 / 0.0065 and contact-free 0.0146 / 0.0078,
       against 0.027.
     - **Reserve confirmed, last resort** (after 172). Its R real wall covers only origin to C2, so a V3 splice needs
       the tube above C2.
3. **Tally re-run** (`logs/v5_eye172_tally.log`, `results/scaleup_tally.json`; the 13:55 copy is kept in
   `results/v5_eye172/`).
   - The counts are unchanged: 27 eligible (ia172 14, Song 12, VMR 1). 26 have all-bins > 0.027 on both sides, 19 have
     contact-free >= 0.027 on both.
   - Pairs still waiting for an eye check went from 5 to 0. No side was rejected.
4. **`results/v5_eligible_final.json`** (`v5_eligible_final.py`):
   - **25 primary**: ia172 13, Song 11, VMR 1. All 25 have all-bins > 0.027 on both sides; 18 have contact-free >= 0.027
     on both.
   - **2 reserves**, in order: ia172_172, then Song 138.
   - Each pair records:
     - source, case and patient key; licence and attributions; scanner and voxel; provenance flags (Siemens 1.0 mm Song
       113, 094, 109, 138)
     - coarseness: contact-free and all-bins, per side, with the pair score
     - the tally flags and the eye verdicts per side
     - per side: trace set, qc file, trace file and **wall files** (ct_seg tag, vtp path in container and Windows form,
       md5, VMTK line, measure.json)
     - the side-use rule.
