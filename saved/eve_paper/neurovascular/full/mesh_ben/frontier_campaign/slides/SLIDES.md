# Slides: CB3 against the ablation, TopBrain v2 and the procedural policy

15 PNG slides, 1920×1080, generated by `monitoring/traj/traj_slides.py` from the campaign records
(`python monitoring/traj/traj_slides.py [--only 04,05] [--half 45]`). CB3 = carotid v3 best checkpoint
2 289 002. The pair slides put the two models on the SAME seed, zoomed ±45 mm into the nook where the
other model fails (the onset of its last stall), with the local vessel wall (collision mesh: the TopBrain
anatomy's `vessel_architecture_collision.obj`, or the real patient's mesh extracted from the training
image into `assets/mesh_ben.obj`), the planned path, both devices, and each episode's progress / slack trace.

Frame conventions: guidewire green, catheter red, planned path orange, target X, vessel wall grey, centerlines
