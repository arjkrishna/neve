(4) FIX SCALING: curv_ahead /10 -> clip 0.5 (net already assigns it high per-unit weight), radius_now/ahead /12 -> /6, target clip 50mm leaves dz a constant flag 86% of the time. (5) FIX at_ostium (dead on procedural meshes — _ostium_branch_idx never matches). (6) DE-CRUTCH TIME+MOMENTUM: ep_step is the single largest policy input and the dominant driver of the (railed) log_std head — consider removing it from the policy prefix (keep in privileged tail for the critic) or coarsening it, and consider feeding EXECUTED rather than commanded last action (or both); this directly targets the converging-to-determinism/railed-action failure mode noted in the run status. Items 1, 5 and the force-accessor fix are pure bugs and safe; item 6 is the highest-leverage behavioral experiment.

## caveats
(1) Saliency is local gradient magnitude on ReLU nets — diffuse shares partly reflect global weight growth (sum grew 30x early->latest), so relative ranks matter more than absolute percentages; permutation/ablation importance could reorder mid-table dims. (2) Buffer states are from explore 287k while the 'latest' policy is from explore 510k — distribution shift means latest-policy saliency is evaluated slightly off-distribution; the buffer-matched mid snapshot (u=131k) shows the same ranking structure, so conclusions are stable. (3) 1024-state sample: means are stable but tail behaviors (mask flags active 3%, forks_wrong nonzero 19%) rest on 30-200 active samples. (4) The buffer contains ~98 interleaved deterministic eval episodes (~<2% of states) — not excluded. (5) last_action saliency could partly be legitimate dynamics information (action smoothing is not automatically bad); the momentum-crutch reading is supported but not proven by the mean|a0| growth. (6) log_std analysis used the pre-squash head; because tanh is railed at the ceiling, effective post-squash input sensitivity is ~0 — the 'ep_step drives exploration schedule' claim describes the raw head, whose gradients are currently squash-suppressed. (7) at_ostium being all-zero was verified in the buffer only up to explore 287k; if the ostium fix landed mid-run it could be alive later (no evidence of that in code). (8) Aux gradient shaping, even when inert on 2 of 4 labels, still contributes the contact-proxy signal at ~1e-6 effective scale — 'effectively off' not 'exactly off'.
