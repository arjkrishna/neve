## verdict
For the stall→retract→re-advance problem, the highest-leverage structural fix is TEACHER-STUDENT with a PRIVILEGED ACTOR, wrapped as a RESIDUAL on your scripted heuristic, with a MULTIMODAL/HYBRID action head replacing the tanh-Gaussian. These three compose into one coherent unlock and each attacks a distinct root cause. (1) Your documented AWAC failure — advantage weights collapse to ~1.0 so the critic's "retract-when-stuck" preference cannot transmit — is a symptom of asking a deployable-obs actor to learn a maneuver whose trigger (accumulated slack/windup/contact) it cannot observe. "Privileged Sensing Scaffolds RL" (Hu, Springer, Rybkin, Jayaraman, 2024) shows empirically that confining privileged info to the CRITIC (your current setup) captures only a fraction of the achievable gap, whereas routing it into the ACTOR recovers ~79% of the o⁻→o⁺ gap and beats asymmetric-critic baselines that used 100M samples with only 250K–5M samples. Training the actor on privileged slack/windup makes retract-when-stuck a near-Markovian, directly-learnable decision; then DAgger-distill to the deployable student — the "Learning by Cheating" (Chen et al., CoRL 2019) / Lee et al. (Science Robotics 2020) recipe, which is a proven, sample-frugal, deterministic-deployable pattern and is your primary unlock. (2) Making the RL a residual on the heuristic dissolves the action-mean-penalty knife-edge: the base supplies competence so the policy never has a frozen cold-start ("engine never starts") and never needs BC to bootstrap; RL only learns corrections, which "What Makes Value Learning Efficient in Residual RL" (Ma et al., 2026) shows constrains the action space and makes value learning markedly easier. (3) The bimodal commit-vs-retract optimum is structurally unrepresentable by one Gaussian mean (pathology #4/#2); a hybrid discrete-mode head {advance, steer, retract-relax, reapproach}+magnitude, or a diffusion/GMM policy, makes the bimodality explicit — CHDP (Liu et al., 2026) diagnoses exactly this and shows diffusion policies beat P-DQN/HyAR/hybrid-SAC on parameterized-action multimodality. Layer HER arclength-subgoal relabeling + a formalized reverse curriculum from stuck states (which you already do informally) to remove reward-shaping fragility, and Frenet/path-frame observations + short recurrence to replace the leaky ep_step time crutch. Avoid a full option-critic/HAC/HIRO/Director hierarchy — fragile outside toy domains and a poor fit to your 1–2M-step/day budget.

## candidates
RANK 1 — TEACHER-STUDENT with PRIVILEGED ACTOR + DAgger distillation (PRIMARY UNLOCK). Papers: "Privileged Sensing Scaffolds Reinforcement Learning" (Hu, Springer, Rybkin, Jayaraman, 2024, arXiv 2405.14853); "Learning by Cheating" (Chen, Zhou, Koltun, Krähenbühl, CoRL 2019); "Learning quadrupedal locomotion over challenging terrain" (Lee, Hwangbo, Wellhausen, Koltun, Hutter, Science Robotics 2020); Miki et al. (Science Robotics 2022); asymmetric baseline: Pinto et al., "Asymmetric Actor Critic for Image-Based Robot Learning," RSS 2018. Mechanism: train the ACTOR (not just the critic) on privileged obs — node velocities, contact proxies, device windup/slack — so the stall-trigger is directly observable and retract-when-stuck becomes a near-Markovian, learnable decision; then DAgger/behavior-clone the privileged teacher into a deployable-obs-only student. FIT: excellent. Directly explains your AWAC advantage-collapse (deployable actor cannot see the slack signal the critic keys on, so advantages carry no transmissible information) and directly attacks the stuck-grinder ceiling. Deterministic-deployable by construction (student is obs-only, distilled to argmax/mean). Sample-frugal: scaffolding recovered ~79% of the privileged gap on 250K–5M samples vs 100M for AAC — fits your 1–2M/day budget. Reuses your existing privileged pipeline and 280k demo buffer (seed DAgger). EFFORT: moderate — you already plumb privileged state to the critic; add a privileged-actor training path + a DAgger distillation loop; the student inherits the teacher's recovery behavior instead of rediscovering it from fading exploration noise. RISK: privileged-student information gap on features the deployable obs genuinely cannot proxy (mitigate with short obs-history/recurrence in the student, or DAgger with on-policy student rollouts).

RANK 2 — RESIDUAL RL on the scripted heuristic base. Papers: "Residual Reinforcement Learning for Robot Control" (Johannink et al., ICRA 2019, arXiv 1812.03201); "Residual Policy Learning" (Silver et al., 2018, arXiv 1812.06298); "What Makes Value Learning Efficient in Residual RL" (Ma, Li, Wang, Liu, Bacon, Tao, 2026, arXiv 2602.10539); "Compliant Residual DAgger" (Xu, Hou, Xin, Liu, Song, 2025) — +64% base-policy success on 4 contact-rich tasks with minimal correction data; domain-matched: "Sample-Efficient Learning with Online Expert Correction for Autonomous Catheter Steering" (Wang, Yao, Lu, Pei, Dong, Ma, Qi, ICRA 2026, arXiv 2602.20216) — base fuzzy controller + structured expert-prior reward + online correction converges in 123 episodes (−25.9% vs SAC), positional error 83.8% of SAC. Mechanism: base = your heuristic path-follower (already 30–100% region-dependent); RL outputs a bounded corrective residual added to the base action. FIT: excellent. Kills the "engine never starts" freeze (residual initialized ~0 ⇒ policy already competent ⇒ no dependence on the fragile action-mean-penalty tuning) and slashes exploration burden under your sample budget; deterministic-deployable (base is deterministic, residual head takes mean). Reuses your #1 asset (heuristic) directly. EFFORT: low-moderate. HANDLING THE HEURISTIC'S DISCRETE MODE STRUCTURE (your explicit question): expose the base's current mode as an actor input, and — critically — size the residual bound large enough to REVERSE sign (a small residual cannot flip a "grind-forward" base into "retract"). If you want the residual to be able to switch the base's mode, pair with Rank 3 (a discrete override head) rather than relying on an unbounded residual. RISK: if the base commits to the wrong mode and the residual is bounded, you re-inherit the stuck-grinder — hence compose with a mode selector, not residual-alone.

RANK 3 — PARAMETERIZED/HYBRID or MULTIMODAL action head (fixes the bimodal pathology at the representation level). Papers: HyAR (Li, Tang, Zheng, Hao et al., ICLR 2022, arXiv 2109.05490) — latent hybrid representation beats P-DQN/PA-DDPG/HHQN/hybrid-SAC, esp. high-dim; P-DQN (Xiong et al., 2018); "Continuous-Discrete RL for Hybrid Control in Robotics" / H-MPO (Neunert et al., CoRL 2019); CHDP (Liu et al., 2026, arXiv 2601.05675) — explicitly shows unimodal Gaussian/deterministic policies cannot represent hybrid-action multimodality and that diffusion policies fix it; peg-in-hole evidence that a hybrid coarse+fine agent (~2750 reward) beats fine-only (~2500) and coarse-only (~1750). Mechanism: discrete maneuver selector {advance, steer, retract-relax, reapproach} + continuous magnitude per mode; OR a diffusion/GMM continuous head that represents the commit-vs-retract bimodality without averaging. FIT: strong specifically for pathology #4 (unimodal mean must average commit vs retract) and #2 (tanh-Gaussian saturation/alpha-whipsaw instability — a mixture/diffusion head sidesteps the sigma-collapse/explosion dynamics entirely). Deterministic-deployable: argmax discrete + mean magnitude, or a distilled one-step/deterministic-mode diffusion. EFFORT: moderate-high — new action head + retraining; changes the AWAC/SAC actor loss. RISK: hybrid-action off-policy learning adds its own tuning; diffusion adds inference cost (must confirm <133ms for 7.5Hz — use few-step/distilled diffusion). Compose as the residual head in Rank 2 so you get mode-switching + competence + representability together.

RANK 4 — GOAL-CONDITIONED + HER with arclength subgoals + REVERSE CURRICULUM from stuck states. Papers: HER (Andrychowicz et al., NeurIPS 2017); "Reverse Curriculum Generation for RL" (Florensa, Held, Wulfmeier, Zhang, Abbeel, CoRL 2017); recent subgoal-relabeling variants (2023–2024, OpenReview m7lBCyROPP / OjCWG58ZyY). Mechanism: goal = arclength-s along your planned path; relabel a failed trajectory that reached s' as a success for goal s', removing dependence on hand-shaped dense reward; reverse curriculum formalizes your restore-from-stuck practice — start distribution seeded at hard stall states and expanded outward (Florensa requires resettability, which you have). FIT: strong for removing reward-shaping fragility (your alpha/mean-penalty knife-edge is partly a reward-scaling artifact) and it exploits your arclength-parameterized centerline asset natively. EFFORT: moderate. RISK: HER teaches WHICH s to reach, not the non-monotone HOW (retract-to-progress) — it must sit on top of Rank 1/3 which supply the action-space structure; pure HER on a unimodal Gaussian will still average the bimodal maneuver.

RANK 5 — SAC-X-style scheduled auxiliary control / privileged auxiliary reward (NOT a full hierarchy). Paper: "Learning by Playing – Solving Sparse Reward Tasks from Scratch" / SAC-X (Riedmiller et al., ICML 2018, arXiv 1802.10567). Mechanism: define "reduce-slack / release-windup" as a first-class auxiliary intention with its own reward computed from your privileged windup/slack signals; schedule it so the agent practices the retract-relax skill as a named behavior rather than hoping exploration noise stumbles into it. FIT: conceptually perfect for the exact maneuver, and you already have the privileged signal to define the auxiliary reward. EFFORT: low if used only as an added auxiliary-reward channel/skill head on your existing off-policy learner; high if you build the full scheduler/option machinery. RISK: full SAC-X scheduling and option-critic/HIRO/HAC/Director hierarchies have thin evidence outside DeepMind's own manipulation suite and are finicky — adopt the auxiliary-reward idea, skip the hierarchy.

RANK 6 — STATE-SPACE STRUCTURE: Frenet/path-frame obs + recurrence to replace the ep_step time crutch. Papers: "Recurrent Model-Free RL Can Be a Strong Baseline for Many POMDPs" (Ni, Eysenbach, Salakhutdinov, ICML 2022); POPGym (Morad et al., ICLR 2023); DRQN lineage. Mechanism: express observations in the path/Frenet frame (position/heading relative to the centerline) for anatomy generalization (removes mesh-specific global coordinates → attacks mesh memorization); replace the global ep_step feature — which you found became the #1 crutch and which encourages TIME-based grinding rather than STATE-based recovery — with either privileged slack obs (Rank 1) or a short observation-history/recurrent encoder that captures stall DURATION as a proper POMDP memory variable. FIT: high value, cheap. Frenet framing is near-free and directly serves your no-mesh-memorization constraint. EFFORT: low (Frenet obs) to moderate (recurrent student). RISK: recurrence adds training cost and can be unnecessary once the privileged-actor/slack obs make the state near-Markovian — prefer short history-stacking over full LSTM unless POMDP residual remains.

(b) "Privileged Sensing Scaffolds RL" (Hu et al., 2024, fetched HTML): Scaffolder routes privileged obs into actor/world-model/exploration, recovers ~79% of the o⁻→o⁺ gap, "outperforms or is competitive with AAC and RMA trained with 100M samples in 9/10 tasks" using 250K–5M samples; ablations show privileged-critic-alone dominates only on some tasks — strongest direct evidence that your critic-only privileged setup is leaving most of the gap unclaimed. (c) CR-DAgger (Xu, Hou, Xin, Liu, Song, 2025, PDF extracted): residual policy on a frozen base + on-policy corrections improves base success by 64% on book-flip/belt/cable/gear-insertion with MINIMAL data, outperforming retrain-from-scratch and finetuning. ## anti_recommendations
(1) FULL hierarchical RL rebuild — option-critic, HIRO, HAC, Director: looks tailor-made for stall→retract→re-advance temporal abstraction, but the literature evidence outside gridworlds/toy control is thin and these are notoriously unstable and sample-hungry; a poor fit to your 1–2M-step/day SOFA budget. Take the SAC-X auxiliary-reward IDEA (a named "reduce-slack" skill using your privileged signal) without the hierarchy/scheduler machinery. (2) Continuing to tune the tanh-Gaussian action-mean penalty (0.005 vs 0.02 knife-edge): the instability is STRUCTURAL — a unimodal squashed Gaussian on a bimodal optimum with SAC entropy/alpha coupling — so more tuning cannot fix sigma-collapse/explosion/alpha-whipsaw; switch the policy CLASS (mixture/diffusion or hybrid discrete head) instead. (3) Pure HER/goal-conditioning as a standalone fix: relabeling reached-s as success removes reward fragility but only teaches WHICH subgoal, not the non-monotone retract-to-progress HOW; on a unimodal Gaussian it still averages commit-vs-retract. Only deploy it layered on the action-space structure. (4) Raw global ep_step / elapsed-time as the stall-duration feature: you already found it becomes the #1 crutch; it encourages time-based grinding and generalizes poorly across anatomies — replace with state-based privileged slack obs or bounded recurrence/history. (5) Residual-RL ALONE with a small residual bound: cannot reverse a base controller that has committed to the wrong (grind) mode — you re-inherit the stuck-grinder ceiling unless you either widen the residual bound to allow sign reversal or add a discrete mode-override head. (6) On-policy PPO-scale or from-scratch flat SAC: ruled out by sample budget and by the stEVE 40/100 baseline showing flat continuous RL plateaus low on this exact task.

## caveats
(1) I could not fetch the Scarponi et al. 2024 "Zero-Shot RL for Autonomous Guidewire Navigation" full text (Springer paywall/redirect; arXiv 2403.02777 exists but I did not extract it) — so I could not verify whether that specific work already uses path-frame obs or curriculum; treat its details as unconfirmed. (2) CHDP and "What Makes Value Learning Efficient in Residual RL" are very recent (2026) preprints; I verified their core claims from the papers but they are not yet peer-reviewed and their benchmark numbers vs your task are not directly comparable — the multimodality/residual mechanisms are the transferable insight, not the absolute numbers. (3) The 7.5 Hz deterministic-deployment constraint is a real risk for a diffusion policy head — I did not find latency numbers proving few-step/distilled diffusion runs <133ms on your box; if diffusion inference is too slow, use a Gaussian-mixture (GMM) head, which also breaks unimodality but is cheap and deterministic-deployable via mode selection. (4) The privileged-actor teacher-student "primary unlock" claim rests strongly on Scaffolder's 79%-of-gap result, which is on manipulation/locomotion benchmarks, not endovascular navigation; the mechanism (make the stall-trigger observable to the actor) is well-motivated for your slack/windup physics, but the magnitude of gain on your task is unverified — I recommend it as the highest-EV bet, not a guarantee. (5) I did not empirically inspect your codebase's current AWAC/critic plumbing or the two local forensic-review PDFs (eve_rl_deep_review_combined.pdf, multimesh design doc) — my "fit" statements rely on the problem description you supplied; if the privileged aux-distillation you already have is stronger than "weak," the incremental gain from a full privileged-actor path shrinks. (6) Residual-RL sim-to-real transfer numbers I cited (≤5% loss, +64% success) are from rigid-robot manipulation; guidewire buckling/slack is a stiffer partial-observability problem, so exploration-burden reduction should transfer but recovery-maneuver quality still depends on the action-head fix.
