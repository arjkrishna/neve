## GAPS — claims with no verified support

1. NEURO-SPECIFIC RADIATION HARM. No source documents cumulative career radiation dose or radiation-attributable harm in neurointerventionalists specifically. The specialty-matched dosimetry (weyland2023dose) and kemerink2002dose both conclude per-procedure operator dose is LOW. SOFTEN — and in fact invert: drop the harm framing and write the dose-SCALING claim the neuro data actually supports (dose rises with fluoroscopy time, DAP, contrast runs, and number of thrombectomy attempts, so fewer/faster passes reduce exposure). Attribute deterministic harm evidence (vano2010cataract) to 'fluoroscopically guided intervention more broadly'. Do NOT cite Roguin 2013 (brain/neck tumors in interventionalists): primary record never retrieved, secondary sources disagree on the case counts (31 vs 36 physicians), and it is an uncontrolled voluntary case series that has been publicly contested.
2. TORTUOSITY PREDICTS ACCESS FAILURE. Not supported. koge2022tortuosity measured outright failure to reach the target at 2.5% vs 0.4% and it was NOT statistically significant. SOFTEN to 'predicts longer procedures and reduced first-pass success'.
3. RECURRENCE IS INSUFFICIENT UNDER HYSTERETIC / STICK-SLIP DYNAMICS. No verified support, and there is direct counter-evidence (ni2022recurrent: recurrent model-free RL matches or beats specialised POMDP methods on 18/21 environments). SOFTEN to the ni2023transformers-supported form ('memory addresses recall, not credit assignment') plus your own observation that the hidden state here is a mechanical quantity — or back it with an ablation in Sec. 4. Do not keep hunting; the literature does not contain this claim.
4. CONCURRENT TWO-MODE (advance vs. recover) INTERFERENCE INSIDE ONE POLICY. No paper matches the precise mechanism. kirkpatrick2017ewc is sequential-task forgetting; schaul2019rayinterference is objective competition via the agent-controlled data distribution. SOFTEN: cite ray interference for the plateau mechanism and present the advance-vs-recover competition as your own measured result (the 86->58->28% hard-recovery decay is your evidence).
5. AWAC WEIGHTS COLLAPSING TOWARD UNIFORM BC. No prior work documents this failure mode. KEEP AS YOUR OWN EMPIRICAL RESULT — phrase strictly as 'in our setting', never as a property of AWAC. nair2020awac / peng2019awr define the family only.
6. QUANTITATIVE VALIDATION OF SOFA + BeamAdapter GUIDEWIRE/CATHETER BEHAVIOR against physical measurement. Does not exist in the literature (searched hard). Closest: korzeniowski2018vcsim3 (1.34 mm mean error, but VCSim3, not SOFA), dequidt2008coils (SOFA group, coils, contact-free rig), karstensen2022porcine (SOFA-trained policy, 100% sim -> 30% ex vivo, i.e. evidence of a GAP). SOFTEN the fidelity sentence; suggested wording in risk notes. Do not keep hunting.
7. BAND-LIMITED SINUSOIDAL DEFORMATION OF A PATIENT-DERIVED CENTERLINE. No published method does this. All verified synthetic-vasculature work either grows trees de novo (schreiner1993cco, kerautret2023opencco, schneider2012arterialtree) or learns a generative model (prabhakar2024vesselgraph). PRESENT AS NOVEL and contrast explicitly — this is the cleanest defensible novelty claim in the Methods.
8. VOXELIZATION + GAUSSIAN SMOOTHING half of the reconstruction pipeline. No canonical citation exists. DESCRIBE, DON'T CITE (marching cubes and decimation are covered).
9. J-TIP GUIDEWIRE GEOMETRY as a standard design (J-curve radius, tip length). No device-design or review reference found; the only retrieved source (Dahl 2026, Neurointervention 21(1):44-52) is a bench SEM coating-damage study that happens to document J-loop shaping of 0.014-in wires. SOFTEN: drop the geometric specificity, or cite the manufacturer IFU for the specific wire modeled.
10. NUMERIC BEND RADIUS (mm) FOR THE ADULT CAVERNOUS GENUA. Not published in any retrieved abstract; zhang2013siphon measures 'curvature radius' but reports no values in its abstract and is paywalled. If the paper states a bend radius, REPORT IT AS A MEASUREMENT OF YOUR OWN MESH, not as a literature value — or open the Zhang full text.
11. THE 2.5-3.4 mm CAROTID LUMEN. No normative source supports it and three contradict it (CCA 6.10-6.52 mm, cervical ICA 4.66-5.11 mm, cavernous ICA 4.27-4.33 mm). Either re-scope the sentence to the distal target vessel (ophthalmic/terminal ICA 2.71-2.88 mm, MCA ~2.5 mm; cite baz2021morphometry + nikoubashman2017catheter), or report 2.5-3.4 mm as a measured property of YOUR generated meshes and note it is narrower than population carotid norms.

## RISKS — claim outruns source, or competitive landscape

1. HERMES ENDPOINT. The primary outcome is an ORDINAL SHIFT of 90-day mRS (adjusted common OR 2.49, 95% CI 1.76-3.53, NNT 2.6, n=1287, FIVE trials) — not a dichotomized mRS 0-2 rate. Two failure modes a stroke reviewer will catch instantly: attributing a 'functional independence rate' to the 2.49 acOR, and writing 'seven trials' (that is a different, later HERMES publication).
2. THE RADIATION SENTENCE CURRENTLY POINTS THE WRONG WAY. The best specialty-matched source (weyland2023dose) concludes operator exposure is 'generally low but very variable' (median 7 uSv/procedure), and kemerink2002dose says the contribution of neurointerventional procedures to occupational dose 'was very small'. A strong harm claim is actively contradicted by the literature you would be citing. Pivot to dose-scaling-with-attempts, which is both supported and is exactly the argument your method needs.
3. SPECIALTY MISMATCH ON HARM EVIDENCE. vano2010cataract measured interventional CARDIOLOGY personnel (RR 3.2 for posterior subcapsular lens changes; median cumulative lens dose 6.0 Sv, a retrospective estimate, not measured dosimetry). Do not let the sentence imply it measured neurointerventionalists.
4. THE 2.5-3.4 mm LUMEN CLAIM IS WRONG AS WRITTEN and is the single highest-probability reviewer catch in the paper. Krejza 2006 (n=500): CCA 6.10-6.52 mm, ICA 4.66-5.11 mm. Baz 2021 (n=70 CTA): cervical ICA 4.85 mm, cavernous ICA 4.27-4.33 mm. The stated range matches only the ophthalmic/terminal ICA and MCA. Fix the text before submission.
5. 'FIRST DUAL-DEVICE AT SIPHON DEPTH' IS DEAD. robertshaw2025safe navigates two devices through the siphon to M1 at 96% across 12 patient-specific vasculatures. Delete any 'first' phrasing. Frame the wedge as a combination not previously assembled (procedural anatomy + online recovery + privileged actor), never as a first or a best.
6. DO NOT MOTIVATE AGAINST THE HEADLINE 96% OR 100%. Those are narrow / not-held-out. The honest held-out multi-anatomy bars are: SAC 37% across ten patient vasculatures (robertshaw2025worldmodel), SAC 36% on held-out vasculatures (the IROS-2026-submitted world-models preprint, arXiv:2604.20151 — preprint, cite with care or omit), HM-MARL 56-80% multi-vasculature, stEVE DualDeviceNav ~40%. Using the 96% as 'the state of the art you improve on' misrepresents the field in the opposite direction and is just as catchable.
7. MOOSA 2025 CUTS AGAINST YOUR ALGORITHM CHOICE. On stEVE DualDeviceNav, tuned: TD3 68% > SAC 58% > PPO 41% > DDPG 24%. A reviewer holding this paper will ask why not TD3. Preempt it in one clause. Note also that its SAC figure (58%) is well above the stEVE paper's 40% — pick one and state which benchmark configuration you mean, or you look like you cherry-picked the lower bar.
8. BARNES 2025 IS A SAME-INSTITUTION EXPECTED CITATION. JHU + UMD (Krieger, Brown, Sochol); 83% on unseen geometries and 75% on an unseen PATIENT-DERIVED geometry — directly comparable to your Sec. 4 patient-derived result. It is a preprint, but omitting it will look worse to a JHU-adjacent reviewer than citing a preprint.
9. BIBLIOGRAPHIC ERROR IN THE INTERNAL PLAN. PAPER_PLAN_NEUROVASC_RL.md (sec.1.1 table and sec.10) merges arXiv:2406.12499 with 'IJCARS 2025, PMC12167253' as one 96% paper. These are TWO different papers: arXiv:2406.12499 -> IJCARS 2024;19(8):1569-1578 (inverse RL); arXiv:2503.24140 / PMC12167253 -> IJCARS 2025;20(6):1077-1086 (safe two-device, 96%, 0.24 N, 12 anatomies). Fix before the reference list is generated. Also: the plan's 'IJCARS 2025' label on the IRL paper is a year error (it is 2024).
10. KIRCHHOFF, NOT COSSERAT. The BeamAdapter README says '1-dimensional Finite Element Method (FEM) based on the Kirchhoff rod theory'. If the Methods says the devices are modeled as Cosserat rods, that is wrong as distributed (Kirchhoff is the inextensible, unshearable special case). The verified Cosserat references (tang2012cosserat, korzeniowski2014cosserat, lenoir2006rod) describe OTHER simulators and must not be presented as describing BeamAdapter.
11. DO NOT WRITE 'OUR SIMULATOR IS VALIDATED.' No validation of SOFA/BeamAdapter guidewire behavior against physical measurement exists. Safe wording: 'Device parameters follow published FEM beam models of interventional devices [duriez2006catheter]; related simulators have been validated against physical instruments in phantoms to ~1.3 mm mean shape error [korzeniowski2018vcsim3], though sim-to-real transfer of learned navigation policies degrades substantially [karstensen2022porcine].' Also: nothing validates that BeamAdapter reproduces hysteretic stick-slip contact, which is precisely the mechanism your partial-observability argument leans on.
12. TWO ASYMMETRIC-AC REFERENCES CUT AGAINST YOUR DESIGN. baisero2022unbiased argues the standard state-conditioned asymmetric critic — most likely the variant you run — is BIASED under partial observability; warrington2021robust shows a privileged expert can teach unsafe actions because 'the expert does not know what the trainee cannot see', which is exactly the privileged-actor teacher -> DAgger student setup. Cite each only with a one-clause acknowledgment, or leave both out. Do not cite them as endorsements. Related: yang2017autonomy names no level 'supervised autonomy' (Level 2 is 'task autonomy') — attributing that phrase to Yang is a checkable error; use shademan2016supervised for the term.
